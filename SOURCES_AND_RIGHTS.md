# Sources, rights and attribution

This register separates the materials used in the private reconstruction from the materials redistributed in the public package.

| Source family | Contribution | Included in repository | Applicable condition | Publication decision |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Historical map scans associated locally with BnF/Gallica provenance | Primary evidence for roads, symbols and labels | No | Gallica generally permits non-commercial reuse of public-domain reproductions with the attribution “Source gallica.bnf.fr / Bibliothèque nationale de France”; partner or protected items can have different conditions | Excluded until each scan is linked to its exact institutional notice |
| Research vector layers | Digitized and georeferenced road and locality objects | Yes | CC BY-NC 4.0, subject to source-specific conditions | Included in version 1.0.0 |
| GeoNames | Contemporary name and coordinate candidates | Raw dump: no; identifiers and selected attributes: yes | Creative Commons Attribution 4.0; credit GeoNames | Attribution included in version 1.0.0 |
| OpenStreetMap | Contemporary road context and comparison networks | No | Open Data Commons Open Database License with attribution to OpenStreetMap contributors | Raw extracts and OSM-derived comparison layers excluded from this map release |
| Research code and annual settings | Reconstruction and packaging logic | Yes | MIT for `code/`; CC BY-NC 4.0 for configurations | Included in version 1.0.0 |

## Attributions for the public release

- GeoNames fields: `Contains geographical names and identifiers from GeoNames, licensed under CC BY 4.0.`
- OpenStreetMap is not redistributed in this release. Any later release of OSM-derived databases must credit `© OpenStreetMap contributors` and identify the ODbL.
- Historical source scans are not redistributed. If they are added in a later release, each scan must be linked to its exact institutional notice and carry the required attribution, including `Source gallica.bnf.fr / Bibliothèque nationale de France` where applicable.

## Source-documentation limitation

The private source archive identifies the 1938 evidence as a BnF map but does not currently preserve exact Gallica item URLs for all six scans. Version 1.0.0 therefore excludes every scan. This limitation does not prevent release of the documented vector package; it prevents adding the scans themselves until the six item notices are recovered or supplied.
