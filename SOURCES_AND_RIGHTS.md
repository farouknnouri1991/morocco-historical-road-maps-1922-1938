# Sources, rights and attribution

This register separates the materials used in the private reconstruction from the materials redistributed in the public package.

| Source family | Contribution | Included in repository | Applicable condition | Publication decision |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Six historical map scans from BnF/Gallica | Primary evidence for roads, symbols and labels | Yes: immutable JPEGs, world files and one tiled 1938 display derivative | Gallica permits non-commercial reuse of these BnF reproductions with the attribution “Source gallica.bnf.fr / Bibliothèque nationale de France”; commercial reuse follows Gallica's licensing terms | Included with exact BnF and Gallica ARKs; scans and raster derivative excluded from the repository CC licence |
| Research vector layers | Digitized and georeferenced road and locality objects | Yes | CC BY-NC 4.0, subject to source-specific conditions | Included since version 1.0.0 |
| GeoNames | Contemporary name and coordinate candidates | Raw dump: no; identifiers and selected attributes: yes | Creative Commons Attribution 4.0; credit GeoNames | Attribution included since version 1.0.0 |
| OpenStreetMap | Contemporary road context and comparison networks | No | Open Data Commons Open Database License with attribution to OpenStreetMap contributors | Raw extracts and OSM-derived comparison layers excluded from this map release |
| Research code and annual settings | Reconstruction and packaging logic | Yes | MIT for `code/`; CC BY-NC 4.0 for configurations | Included since version 1.0.0 |
